## 🌟 Overview: More Than a Trainer—A Strategic Companion

The Spire Almanac is not just another modification toolkit; it's a **strategic co-pilot** for players who want to deconstruct, analyze, and master the intricate mechanics of their favorite deck-building roguelike. Imagine having a **battlefield cartographer** that maps every possible combat outcome, a **treasury auditor** that helps you reallocate resources, and a **personal scoring coach**—all fused into one elegant, hotkey-driven overlay.

This project reimagines the concept of a "trainer" by focusing on **educational gameplay enhancement**. Instead of simply giving you an advantage, it provides the **diagnostic tools** to understand *why* certain strategies work, allowing you to become a better player even when the toolkit is inactive. It's the difference between being handed the answer key and being taught the mathematical principles behind the equations.

## 🎯 Core Philosophy: The "Gameplay Microscope"

Think of this toolkit as a **high-powered lens** placed over your gaming session. It doesn't just change numbers; it reveals the hidden **causal relationships** between your choices, your deck composition, and the final score. Our philosophy is built on three pillars:

1.  **Transparency:** Every modification is logged and reversible, turning your experimental runs into **documented case studies**.
2.  **Precision:** Real-time adjustments are surgical, not blunt. You tweak one variable at a time to measure its isolated impact.
3.  **Reversibility:** The "Chrono-Seal" feature lets you rewind a specific gameplay moment, encouraging **risk-free experimentation** with bizarre deck builds.

## ✨ Key Features: A Suite of Analytical Instruments

### ⚔️ Combat Resolution Engine
This isn't a simple "one-hit-kill" toggle. Instead, the **Combat Resolution Engine** allows you to micro-manage the **damage formula** on the fly. You can adjust your *Strength scaling* by incremental multipliers or fine-tune your *Block efficiency* to simulate hypothetical relic combinations. It’s designed for **theory-crafting** extreme builds.

### 🛡️ Defensive Matrix Tuning
Adjust the **integrity** of your defensive layers. This feature focuses on the *kinetics* of combat—you can alter the *intensity* of enemy debuffs or the *potency* of your vulnerability application. This is perfect for stress-testing a "glass cannon" archetype to find its breaking point.

### 💰 Economic Stability Modulator
The **Economic Stability Modulator** is a granular tool for adjusting the *mercantile flow* within the Spire. Instead of adding a flat amount, you can adjust the *interest rate* on your gold reserves or modify the *value multiplier* of specific relic sells. This helps you understand the **intrinsic economy** of the game.

### 🏆 Score Augmentation Calculator
This is a **meta-analytical tool**. Rather than adding points, it recalculates your *performance index* based on dynamic variables like *completion speed* and *resource efficiency*. It encourages **speed-running strategies** and optimization of the "Perfect Run" metric.

### ⌨️ The Hotkey "Gesture" System
Our hotkey system is context-aware. The "Gesture" system allows you to record a sequence of modifications (e.g., "Increase Strength by 2, Gain 5 Block, Draw 1 Card") and bind it to a single keystroke. This facilitates **complex macro-strategies** that would otherwise require multiple pauses.

## 🚀 Getting Started: Integration & Setup

To integrate the Almanac into your environment, follow this "Echo-Link" process.

### Step 1: The "Seismic" Install
The toolkit uses a **non-invasive overlay method**. It attaches to the game's memory stream without modifying core files. To install, download the archive and extract it to your preferred **Tools Directory**. Run the `Almanac_Launcher` executable. It will automatically detect the game instance.

### Step 2: First-Time Calibration
Upon first launch, the Almanac will run a **"Dimensional Scan"** to map the memory addresses. This takes less than 10 seconds. Once calibrated, the main Dashboard (accessible via `F10`) will populate with real-time data.

### Step 3: The "Safe-Harbor" Protocol
The Almanac automatically creates a **"Hypothesis Save"** before you make any major modification. This ensures that any experiment can be reverted, preventing soft-locks in the Spire.
